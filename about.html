Our Story

Coffee Roaster on St Martin's, Isles of Scilly

Hannah started roasting coffee on St Martin's with a sack of green beans bought from a trading ship travelling from Colombia to Cornwall. She roasted the first batches over a fire on the beach with her island friends.

Since then, Hannah learned about sourcing and roasting, supplying organic coffee to the Island Bakery where people began asking to take bags home with them, which encouraged her to start packing small batches of coffee in pouches.

Hannah met Minji in 2025, and they became best friends. Minji was hearing about Hannah's coffee roasting one day and remembered there are coffee beans grown in China too, where Minji is from. Minji sourced some Yunnan beans, Hannah roasted, and they tried it together in a sunny winter morning on island. They fell in love with the flavour of Yunnan Coffee and want more people to try it too.

Alone with the coffee beans, tea lover Minji also sourced some tea she's found of from different regions of China and shared those with Hannah and her friends. They quickly abandoned tea bags and embraced those different shapes of tea from all over the China.

Now, Hannah and Minji are on the journey to share the drinks they love to more people, and have been spreading coffee beans and tea from China ever since.
