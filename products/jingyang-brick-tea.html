Jingyang Brick Tea

Golden Flowers Earthy Sweet Complex

A traditional compressed Fu Brick tea from Jingyang in Shaanxi — widely considered the birthplace of Fu Brick Tea. Dense golden flowers (Eurotium cristatum fungus) within the brick create a complex, sweet, earthy character with remarkable depth. A piece of Chinese tea history.

TypeFu Brick (compressed)
Golden FlowersYes
OriginJingyang, Shaanxi, China
Brewing Guide

Break off a small section (around 5g) with a tea knife (cha dao). Rinse with boiling water, then steep at 95°C for 60–90 seconds. Re-steep 5–7 times. Ideal with milk.
