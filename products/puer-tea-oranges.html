Pu-er Tea Oranges

Earthy Sweet Orange Citrus Finish

Aged pu-erh tea packed inside dried whole mandarin orange peel. Over time, the citrus infuses the tea — creating an earthy, sweet cup with a distinctively bright orange finish. Each piece makes a striking gift and a wonderful daily brew. A masterpiece of Chinese tea craft.

TypeAged Pu-erh in orange peel
FormatWhole dried mandarin
OriginYunnan, China
Brewing Guide

Break the orange peel to release the tea leaves. Rinse with boiling water (95°C), then steep 45–90 seconds. Re-steep 4–5 times. Can also be simmered gently in water for a stronger brew.
