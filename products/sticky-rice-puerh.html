Sticky Rice Pu-erh Tea

Earthy Sticky Rice Honeyed Warm

Small compressed tea balls infused with the comforting aroma of sticky rice (nuomi). Each ball brews multiple cups — earthy pu-erh character with warm honeyed rice fragrance that deepens through infusions. A deeply satisfying and aromatic pu-erh experience.

TypePu-erh (compressed)
FormatSmall tea balls
OriginYunnan, China
Brewing Guide

Use one ball per gaiwan or teapot. Rinse briefly with boiling water (95°C), then steep 30–60 seconds. Re-steep 4–6 times, increasing steeping time with each round.
