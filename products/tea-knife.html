Tea Knife (Cha Dao)

Essential Tool Stainless Steel Wooden Handle

A stainless-steel cha dao (tea knife) for prying apart compressed pu-erh cakes and brick teas. The pointed blade and ergonomic wooden handle make it easy to extract leaves without crumbling the brick. Essential for anyone working with compressed teas.

MaterialStainless steel blade
HandleWood
UseCompressed tea (pu-erh cakes, bricks)
Brewing Guide

Insert the tip of the cha dao into the edge of a compressed tea brick or cake. Gently lever to pry off a small section. Work along the layers — never force directly into the centre.
